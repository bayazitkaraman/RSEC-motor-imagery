SUPPORTING RESULTS INDEX
Checked October 2, 2026. Paths below are relative to revision_support/data/.

MAIN AND OMITTED COMPARISONS
primary_statistics.csv: four primary contrasts, including real-minus-surrogate.
statistics_54.csv: six features x three bands x three contrasts; Table 2.
paired_effect_comparisons_45.csv: five baselines x three bands x three contrasts.
  Includes all 15 execution-rest comparisons omitted from Figure 4, as well as
  the 30 imagery-related comparisons displayed there. Intervals use the full
  45-comparison family, not separate 15- or 30-comparison families.
sensitivity_statistics.csv: seven settings x three contrasts (21 tests).
  Includes the seven execution-rest tests omitted from Table 3; all 21 tests
  remain in the original Holm family.
cohort_sensitivity.csv: five cohort/sampling-rate configurations x three contrasts.
low_frequency_sensitivity_12.csv: four connectivity features x three contrasts
  when the lowest Fourier bin is excluded.

SPATIAL AND PARTICIPANT RESULTS
primary_subject_summary.csv: participant-level primary condition summaries.
imagery_node_deltas_by_subject.csv: participant/channel imagery-rest differences.
imagery_channel_statistics.csv: 64 channel-level tests and their correction.
imagery_roi_statistics.csv: frontopolar, sensorimotor and between-region tests.

ARTIFACT AND AGGREGATION RESULTS
amplitude_aggregation_statistics.csv: all nine aggregation/rejection settings,
  including all-channel and frontopolar limits of 150, 250 and 500 microvolts,
  removal of the top 20% by either channel set, and median aggregation. Select
  metric=rsec for the 27 RSEC setting/contrast rows described in the paper.
  matched_original_mean_delta and matched_original_dz refer to the SAME retained
  participants BEFORE amplitude-based rejection; both conditions of this
  comparison retain the 1-31-Hz preprocessing. They do not mean unfiltered EEG.
csd_statistics.csv: surface-Laplacian results; metric=rsec gives three contrasts.

The artifact and CSD files also retain historical linear-feature rows for source
provenance. Those rows are not manuscript baselines. Original correction-family
columns are preserved; deleting other rows does not justify recomputing a smaller
family. The manuscript reports RSEC artifact/CSD effects descriptively.
Artifact masks were evaluated using GLOBAL density, not regional participation
scores. The regional participant consistency analysis is a separate result.

REPRODUCIBILITY AND ACCESS
DATA_MANIFEST.json records original source paths and SHA256 hashes for all 12 CSVs.
build_assets.py regenerates three tables and two figures from those saved results.
validate_revision.py checks numerical entries, supporting-result coverage,
preserved assets, terminology and the compiled clean/red PDFs.
These are saved-results and figure/table regeneration materials, not a complete
raw-EEG analysis environment. No raw EEG recordings are redistributed.
The public repository location for the source archive and compiled manuscripts is:
https://github.com/bayazitkaraman/RSEC-motor-imagery/tree/main/docs/spmb_2026
The extracted CSV files are also under results/summary/spmb_2026/ in that repository.
